## Canonical character reference

Use case: stylized concept. Transparent, full-body, front-facing youthful pixel-art sprite. Preserve Bar’s apparent age, short tousled dark-brown hair, large grey-green eyes, small nose, recognizable facial proportions, and slim build. No background, text, extra characters, outline or sparkles. Image generation used the original photo only as an identity reference.

Cell order: (1) driving a small red car with bunny, fox and panda racers; (2) seated at a table eating chicken, cucumber and eggs, milk glass nearby; (3) spacesuit Bar at a rocket cockpit, operating a glowing number-battery control beside a moon; (4) goalkeeper Bar diving to catch a soccer ball; (5) Bar fishing in a small boat with rod, line and cat; (6) Bar hammering a treehouse ladder plank beside a squirrel; (7) explorer Bar digging on a pirate island beside a treasure chest, map and crab; (8) chef Bar balancing pancakes for admiring animal customers; (9) Bar riding a friendly baby dinosaur with a larger smiling dinosaur nearby. Distinct poses/props, full context, no text, symbols, numbers or watermarks. Crop into nine separate reviewable runtime scenes.
